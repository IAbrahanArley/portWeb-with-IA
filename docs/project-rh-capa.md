Paleta extraída do CSS: #126a5b, #172026 e #f4f7f6.

Imagem conceitual gerada com a ferramenta integrada image_gen; não é uma captura da aplicação. Arquivo servido localmente: frontend/public/projects/project-rh-contexto.webp. ## Prompt final

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Landscape portfolio project cover, 1536x1024 composition, for a Brazilian internal recruitment web application named project-rh.
Primary request: Create an elegant editorial 3D illustration representing internal recruitment: employee CVs connect to an open job opportunity, and one application progresses through review to approval. Base the concept on actual implemented features: job vacancies, candidate resumes, HR review and application status tracking.
Scene/backdrop: A quiet pale sage studio surface, subtle paper texture, soft natural shadows. A single sculptural deep forest green architectural arch stands behind a large off-white job vacancy card, a visual doorway to a new career opportunity. Three smaller physical resume cards approach the opening in a composed shallow arc, connected by a precise thin green path. Use a simple embossed human profile medallion and restrained typographic rules on the resume cards. A small restrained green check seal on the selected resume conveys approval.
Style/medium: Art-directed tactile paper and ceramic 3D still life, sophisticated software editorial illustration, crisp edges, matte surfaces, credible lighting, beautiful restraint.
Composition/framing: Landscape image with a strong central composition, everything essential within the middle 75% so it survives portfolio card cropping. Camera gently elevated, enough depth to see tangible layers. A large vacancy card is the main focal point; smaller CV cards give scale and narrative. Plenty of breathing room. Artwork fills the image; no outer border.
Color palette: Source application palette: forest green #126a5b, near-black #172026, pale gray-green #f4f7f6, white and muted sage. Warm soft light.
Text (verbatim): Only two clear short labels, "Vaga aberta" on the main vacancy card and "Currículo" on the foremost resume card. Other details use simple abstract printed rules, no gibberish.
Constraints: This is a conceptual cover illustration, not a purported screenshot. No computer or phone mockup, no floating dashboard panels, no graphs, no hiring statistics, no feature claims, no brand logos, no technology logos, no watermark, no stock office scene, no robot, no neon, no purple gradients, no excessive icons. Professional readable Portuguese lettering.
```
